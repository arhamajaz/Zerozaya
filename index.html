Our Story

About ZeroZaya ℹ️

"Zaya" means waste. We are ZeroZaya — Zero Waste. Our mission: no food left behind.

💡

Why This Matters

India wastes 40% of its food — worth ₹92,000 crore annually. Yet 19 crore Indians face hunger daily. The solution is redistribution, not scarcity.

🤝

How We Work

Volunteer-first and community-driven. Donors list food, volunteers pick it up and deliver to NGOs and individuals. No middleman. No profit.
